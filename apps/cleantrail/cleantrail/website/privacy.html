先说重点

CleanTrail 当前版本无需账号，在设备上完成导入、检查、修复、预览与导出，不建立开发者云端数据库，也未集成广告、行为分析或跨应用追踪。只有你主动选择分享目标时，导出文件才会离开 CleanTrail。

1. 适用范围与处理主体

本政策适用于 CleanTrail（清迹）移动应用及其官方静态网站。具体功能以你实际安装的版本为准。

2. 隐私摘要

  • 无需账号：当前版本不提供注册、登录、用户档案或开发者云端空间。
  • 本机处理：表格解析、质量检查、修复、复检、预览和报告生成均在设备上完成。
  • 不覆盖源文件：你从文件提供者选择的原始文件不会被 CleanTrail 原位改写。
  • 由你分享：清洗 CSV 与 Markdown 报告只在你点击导出后交给系统分享面板。
  • 无广告与追踪：当前项目代码未集成广告 SDK、行为分析 SDK、崩溃上传 SDK 或跨应用追踪。

4. 功能如何处理信息

4.1 导入与格式确认

只有你主动打开系统文件选择器并选择文件后，CleanTrail 才读取该文件。文本文件支持 CSV/TSV 和 UTF-8/GBK 识别与手动确认；XLSX 需要选择工作表。应用会拒绝不支持或超过当前规模限制的文件。

4.2 本机检查、修复与复检

CleanTrail 在设备上检查缺失值、规范化后的重复行、首尾空格、数字列类型混杂和日期格式不一致或无效。每个问题由你选择修复、替换、删除重复行或保留原样；应用不会把表格发送给服务器，也不会使用在线 AI 自动作出决定。

4.3 本地项目与恢复

应用私有项目文件包含源数据快照、当前工作副本、检测问题和审计动作。保存新版本时会使用临时文件并保留最近有效备份，用于中断后的本地恢复。该备份不是云备份。

4.4 预览与导出

前五行表格和首个可用数值列的校验图在设备上生成。导出时，CleanTrail 在专用临时目录生成 CSV 与 Markdown 报告；报告包含文件名、数量、质量分和操作摘要，但不嵌入完整原始行。文件随后交给系统分享面板。

6. 共享与第三方处理

我们不出售个人信息，也不为广告共享表格数据。当前版本没有开发者账号服务器，因此开发者不会日常接收你的表格、问题记录或审计动作。

以下情况下，其他主体可能按其自身规则处理相关信息：

  • 文件提供者：你选择的系统 Files、网盘或其他文件提供者负责把文件交给应用。
  • 分享目标：只有你选择目标后，导出文件才会交给对应应用、联系人、文件位置或云服务。
  • 设备与操作系统服务：系统设备备份、设备迁移或你启用的云端设备备份可能复制应用数据。
  • 应用商店与分发渠道：渠道可能独立处理下载、更新、商店分析或系统诊断；CleanTrail 当前未接入开发者自建分析服务。
  • 网站基础设施：部署后，托管、DNS 或网络防护服务可能处理正常的连接与安全日志。

7. 保存与删除

  • 本地项目、工作副本、问题与审计动作会保留到你在应用中移除项目、清除应用数据或卸载应用。
  • 移除项目时，CleanTrail 会删除当前项目、临时写入文件和最近有效恢复备份；原始文件仍由原文件位置管理。
  • 语言与引导偏好会保留到应用数据被清除或应用被卸载。
  • 导出文件会在系统分享面板返回后分别尝试删除，并在下次启动或导出前再次清理专用临时目录。若应用被强制终止，临时副本可能保留到下一次清理。
  • 你已经分享出去的文件由目标位置、接收者或服务保存，需在对应位置另行删除。
  • 设备备份或迁移副本受你的设备与平台设置控制，并不属于 CleanTrail 开发者云同步。

8. 本网站与外部链接

本网站是静态页面，不设置广告 Cookie 或跨站追踪器。语言选择保存在浏览器 localStorage 的 cleantrail-site-language 中，网页代码不会把该值发送给开发者。浏览器请求页面时，网络和最终托管基础设施仍可能产生正常的 IP 地址、时间、浏览器类型与错误日志。

点击邮箱链接会交给设备的邮件应用；你通过邮件发送的内容由邮件服务处理。浏览器、邮件服务、应用商店和其他外部目标遵循其自身隐私规则。

9. 你的控制与权利

核心数据默认由你在设备上持有。你可以直接：

  • 查看修复队列、预览表格，并决定修复、替换、删除重复行或保留原样；
  • 在当前会话撤销最近决定，或移除整个本地项目；
  • 不使用导出功能，或只把文件分享给你信任的目标；
  • 清除应用数据或卸载应用，以删除设备上的应用私有数据，并单独处理已分享副本与系统备份；
  • 清除本站浏览器存储，以移除网站语言偏好；以及
  • 通过下方邮箱提出隐私问题或行使适用法律规定的权利。

由于开发者当前不持有应用内本地项目数据，通常无法从服务器替你检索、导出、修改或恢复表格记录。

10. 安全与数据边界

本地优先设计减少数据离开设备的机会，但不能消除设备丢失、弱密码、越狱/Root、恶意软件、误分享、不可信外部存储或他人查看导出文件等风险。请使用设备锁，并根据表格敏感程度选择可信的分享目标和存储位置。

系统文件提供者、设备备份、分享目标和托管服务的安全性不由 CleanTrail 控制。质量分和问题检测是启发式数据检查，不构成数据准确性、合规性或专业审计认证。

11. 政策更新

如果应用增加账号、开发者云同步、在线备份、协作、在线 AI、广告、分析、订阅、网络处理或其他改变数据流的功能，我们会在发布前重新评估并更新本政策。重要更新可能通过应用、官网、商店版本说明或法律要求的方式通知。

4. How features process information

4.1 Import and format confirmation

CleanTrail reads a file only after you open the system picker and select it. Text files support CSV/TSV and UTF-8/GBK detection with manual confirmation; XLSX requires worksheet selection. Unsupported or over-limit files are rejected.

4.2 On-device inspection, repair, and reinspection

CleanTrail checks missing values, normalized duplicate rows, surrounding whitespace, mixed types in numeric columns, and inconsistent or invalid dates on your device. You decide whether to repair, replace, remove a duplicate, or keep the original. The app does not send tables to a server or use online AI to decide for you.

4.3 Local project and recovery

The app-private project file contains the source snapshot, current working copy, detected issues, and audit actions. A temporary file and the last valid backup help recover from interrupted local writes. This is not cloud backup.

4.4 Preview and export

The first-five-row table and numeric validation preview are generated on-device. Export creates a CSV and Markdown report in a dedicated temporary directory. The report contains the filename, counts, score, and action summary but not complete source rows. Files are then passed to the system share sheet.

6. Sharing and third-party processing

We do not sell personal information or share table data for advertising. The current version has no developer account server, so the developer does not routinely receive your tables, issue records, or audit actions.

Other parties may process information in these situations:

  • File providers: the system Files app, cloud drive, or other provider you choose supplies the file to CleanTrail.
  • Share destinations: exported files reach the app, contact, location, or cloud service you select.
  • Device and operating-system services: system backup, device migration, or cloud device backup you enable may copy app data.
  • App stores and distribution channels: a channel may independently handle downloads, updates, store analytics, or system diagnostics. CleanTrail currently includes no developer-operated analytics service.
  • Website infrastructure: hosting, DNS, or network-protection providers may handle normal connection and security logs after deployment.

7. Retention and deletion

  • The local project, working copy, issues, and audit actions remain until you remove the project in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Removing a project deletes the current project, temporary write file, and last valid recovery backup. Your original source file remains under its original file provider.
  • Language and onboarding preferences remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • Export files are individually deleted after the share sheet returns, and the dedicated temporary directory is cleaned again at the next launch or export. If the app is forcibly terminated, temporary copies may remain until that next cleanup.
  • Files you already shared are controlled by the destination, recipient, or service and must be deleted there.
  • Device-backup or migration copies are controlled by your device and platform settings and are not CleanTrail developer cloud sync.

8. This website and external links

This is a static website. It sets no advertising cookies or cross-site trackers. Language selection is stored in browser localStorage under cleantrail-site-language, and the site code does not send that value to the developer. Normal IP address, time, browser-type, and error logs may still arise in network and final hosting infrastructure when your browser requests a page.

Email links are handed to your mail app, and anything you send is handled by your email provider. Browsers, email services, app stores, and other external destinations follow their own privacy rules.

9. Your controls and rights

You hold core data locally by default. You can directly:

  • review the repair queue and table preview, then repair, replace, remove a duplicate, or keep the original;
  • undo recent decisions in the current session or remove the complete local project;
  • avoid export or share files only with destinations you trust;
  • clear app data or uninstall the app to remove app-private data from the device, while handling shared copies and system backups separately;
  • clear this site's browser storage to remove the website language preference; and
  • contact us below with a privacy question or to exercise an applicable legal right.

Because the developer does not currently hold your local project data, we normally cannot search, export, change, or restore your table records from a server.

10. Security and data boundaries

Local-first design reduces opportunities for data to leave the device, but it cannot eliminate risks such as a lost device, weak passcode, jailbreak/root access, malware, accidental sharing, untrusted external storage, or someone viewing exported files. Use a device lock and choose trusted destinations based on the sensitivity of your tables.

CleanTrail does not control the security of system file providers, device backups, share destinations, or hosting providers. Quality scores and issue detection are heuristic data checks, not a guarantee of accuracy, compliance, or professional audit certification.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app adds accounts, developer cloud sync, online backup, collaboration, online AI, advertising, analytics, subscriptions, network processing, or another feature that changes data flows, we will reassess and update this policy before release. Material changes may be announced in the app, on the website, in store release notes, or as required by law.
