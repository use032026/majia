Privacy Policy

This draft describes the ReaderEdit 1.0.0 implementation inspected on 16 October 2026. The operator, monitored support address, production domain, and effective date have not been confirmed. It is not a published or legally reviewed policy.

Information and data

ReaderEdit handles novel and chapter titles, descriptions, text you type or import, separate revision sessions, reader promises, paragraph signals, notes, revised passages, and completion checks. These are stored in the app’s local workspace. Language, appearance, and tour-completion preferences are also stored locally. No account, app-owned backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, payment, or AI processing service is implemented in the inspected code. Manuscript and revision content is not sent to an app-operated server by that code.

Device permissions

Text import opens the operating system’s file picker and reads the file you select. It does not request general access to your file library. The inspected iOS target has no camera, photos, microphone, contacts, location, speech, or notification usage prompts. The Android main manifest includes INTERNET permission for optional web content; the reviewed app does not request runtime access to those sensors or personal-data libraries. A system or file provider may show its own access or authentication prompts.

Network and third-party services

Writing, reading, revision, and decoding selected UTF-8 or BOM-marked UTF-16 files run on the device. The app uses Flutter, file_selector, path_provider, shared_preferences, and webview_flutter. These dependencies provide interface, file selection, storage, and web rendering. No production privacy URL is configured by default. When an HTTPS policy URL is supplied at build time, opening Privacy policy loads that page in the system WebView with JavaScript disabled. The website host and network providers can then receive ordinary request information such as IP address and requested page, and WebView behavior is governed by the operating system. Redirects and services on the final hosted page require a separate review. Cloud file providers may download selected files under your provider settings. ReaderEdit has no app-managed cloud sync. System backups or device migration may copy app data under your operating-system settings; this is separate from app-managed sync. This generated website stores only your language choice in browser local storage. It includes no analytics script, advertising script, external font, or third-party image request. Final hosting logs and services must be reviewed before publication.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Manuscript and revision records remain in the app sandbox until you delete the relevant records or erase all manuscript and revision data in Settings. The app maintains local primary, recovery, and pending snapshots to reduce interrupted-save loss. Successful deletion updates app-managed recovery snapshots so deleted records are not intentionally restored by that recovery path. Novel or chapter deletion does not remove separately created revision sessions. Erase all local data clears novels and revision sessions while language, appearance, and tour preferences remain. Uninstall and system backup retention depend on your operating system; deletion here does not remove the original imported file, system backups, or copies you have made outside the app. The app does not claim secure forensic erasure.

Exports and sharing

Copy revised draft and Copy revision log write the chosen text to the system clipboard only when you tap them. Other apps and clipboard-sync services may access or retain clipboard content according to system permissions and settings. ReaderEdit does not automatically publish your writing, and the inspected code does not read the clipboard. Import creates a local working copy and leaves the original file unchanged. Copies you paste, share, or save elsewhere are controlled by you and the receiving service.

Children's privacy

The inspected app is a general writing workspace and does not request age or date of birth. It has no account or developer-operated service for receiving children’s manuscript data. Intended audience, launch regions, and any region-specific requirements for children must be confirmed before this policy is published. This draft does not establish an age rating or parental-consent process.

Changes to this policy

Before publication, confirm the operator, support contact, domain, effective date, final SDK inventory, hosting behavior, and legal wording. If product data handling changes, the published policy and date should be updated to match. This local draft makes no claim that a notice has already been delivered to users.
