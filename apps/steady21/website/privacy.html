Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current Steady21 app. It is a personal journal for behavior experiments. It has no account system or app-operated cloud sync. The content you enter stays in the app's local storage unless you choose to share a badge through your device's share sheet.

Information you enter

You can enter an experiment name, cue, standard and minimum actions, practice-day goal, daily result, obstacles, restart choices, milestone decisions, and free-text notes. The app also saves your language choice and onboarding state. These records are stored in a JSON file in the app's sandbox. Avoid adding sensitive personal information that you do not need for your own journal.

Collection and services

The reviewed app source has no app-operated server for your journal, and its current dependency list has no advertising or analytics SDK. The app does not send journal records to a developer server. The in-app privacy page opens this hosted policy when available and shows a local summary if it cannot load. This website saves your language selection in browser local storage until you clear that storage. Opening the hosted page sends a request to wowsteady.com; the hosting provider may process technical request data and logs. Hosting and log-retention details remain under review.

Device permissions

The current app does not request camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, location, health, or notification permission for its journal features. Badge sharing uses the system share sheet when you choose it. Operating-system services involved in sharing and backup are controlled by your device settings and the recipient you select.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Your journal remains in local app storage until you delete an experiment, choose “Delete all local data” in Settings, or remove the app. Deleting all local data removes experiments and entries but keeps your language choice. The app cannot restore deleted records. System backups may include app data depending on your platform and device settings. Steady21 has no independent journal backup, cloud sync, or device-transfer feature.

Exports and sharing

The app has no journal export feature. If you choose “Share badge,” Steady21 creates an image of the earned badge and passes it to the system share sheet. A service or person you select may receive that image and handle it under their own terms. Your experiment notes and full journal are not included in the badge image.

Children

Steady21 is a general personal journal and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask for a child's identity or provide a child account. A child using the app could enter free-text content locally; a parent or guardian should review device sharing and backup settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or services change. The page shows its effective date. The operator is an independent developer (独立开发者). For privacy or support questions, email postmaster@hndige.com.

隐私政策

本政策说明当前版本的微成 Steady21 如何处理数据。它是个人行为实验记录册，没有账号系统，也不提供由应用运营方运行的云同步。除非你主动通过设备分享面板分享徽章，输入的内容会保存在应用本地存储中。

你输入的信息

你可以填写实验名称、触发线索、标准与最低行动、目标练习天数、每日结果、阻碍、重启选择、阶段决定和自由文本备注。应用还保存语言选择和引导完成状态。这些记录以 JSON 文件形式存储在应用沙盒中。请勿在日记中加入不必要的敏感个人信息。

数据收集与服务

已检查的应用源码没有处理日记内容的运营方服务器，当前依赖列表也没有广告或分析 SDK。应用不会将实验记录发送给开发者服务器。应用内隐私页在可用时打开此在线政策；无法加载时会显示本地摘要。本网站会在浏览器本地存储语言选择，直到你清除该存储。打开在线页面会向 wowsteady.com 发出请求；托管服务商可能处理技术性请求数据和日志。托管及日志保留细节仍待核对。

设备权限

当前应用的记录功能不请求相机、相册、麦克风、通讯录、位置、健康或通知权限。只有在你主动分享徽章时才会调用系统分享面板。分享和系统备份涉及的系统服务取决于设备设置及你选择的接收方。

存储、保留与删除

记录会保存在本机应用存储中，直至你删除某个实验、在设置中选择“删除全部本地数据”，或移除应用。“删除全部本地数据”会清除实验和逐日记录，但保留语言选择。应用无法恢复已删除的记录。系统备份是否包含应用数据取决于平台和设备设置。微成不提供独立的日记备份、云同步或换机迁移功能。

导出与分享

应用没有完整日记导出功能。如果你选择“分享徽章”，应用会生成已获得徽章的图片，并交给系统分享面板。你选择的服务或接收人可能获得该图片，并按其规则处理。徽章图片不包含实验备注或完整日记。

儿童隐私

微成是通用的个人记录册，并非专为儿童设计。应用不要求儿童身份信息，也不提供儿童账号。儿童使用时仍可能在本机输入自由文本内容；监护人应检查设备的分享和备份设置。

变更与联系

如果应用行为或服务变化，本政策可能更新；页面会显示生效日期。运营主体为独立开发者。如有隐私或支持问题，请发送邮件至 postmaster@hndige.com。
