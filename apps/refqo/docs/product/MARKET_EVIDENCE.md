# Market evidence — 2026-10-21
Research is public product-page inspection, not competitor installation, customer interviews or market-size proof. No screenshots, art, text, datasets or implementation were copied.

| Source | Direct observation | Product implication (inference) |
|---|---|---|
| [Hearthio](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/hearthio/id6804913721), same App ID as supplied China URL | Broad household item profiles, maintenance schedules, checklists, replacement/material/cost history, CSV/ZIP; offline, no account, Chinese and English | These features alone cannot establish originality. | [Itemtopia](https://www.itemtopia.com/home-inventory-app/), [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/itemtopia-inventory/id952405279) | Inventory, receipts, parts, repair records, sharing; current release advertises offline create/edit then sync | Offline + export + localization are constraints, not exclusive market features. |
| [FilterLog](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/filterlog/id6763735977) | Per-filter cadence, spares, replacement decrement, low-stock reminders, paid sharing/barcode | Simple filter inventory is insufficient. Refqo must demonstrate pooled stock across appliances and horizon-based quantities. |
| [Filtro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/filtro-filter-tracker/id6789993291) | Replacement tracking, spare inventory, widgets, reorder reference in paid tier | No claim that spare tracking or model storage is new. |

Observed public descriptions do not explicitly demonstrate the full supply-first, many-appliance pooled-demand → net purchase gap → receipt → replace → undo loop. This is a differentiation hypothesis, not proof that competitors lack it. There are no user interviews, verified recurring complaints or commercial demand evidence. Conduct household usability testing before publication.
