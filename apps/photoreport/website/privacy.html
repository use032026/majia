01

适用范围

本政策适用于 iPhone、iPad 和 Android 上的 JOVFX（下称“本 App”）及本静态产品官网。本 App 用于把现场照片、标注、编号与说明整理成记录。

使用核心功能不需要创建账号，也无需提供姓名、手机号或电子邮箱。你在项目或记录中主动填写的姓名、联系人等内容属于你选择记录的业务内容，并非本 App 的注册资料。

本政策适用于 1.0 版。项目记录和导出文件默认存储在你的设备上。

03

存储与用途

本 App 将项目、记录、照片和标注存于设备的应用沙盒中。当前提供的记录、标注和导出功能无需账号或应用自有的云同步服务，也没有将记录发送到开发者服务器的流程。

本 App 仅为以下目的在设备上处理信息：

  • 创建、编辑、搜索与展示项目和现场记录；
  • 导入、复制、归类并标注处理前与处理后照片；
  • 为项目内记录分配稳定编号并保存状态、责任与期限；
  • 在设备上生成、预览与再次打开 PDF；
  • 按你的指令创建或恢复本地备份；
  • 保存显示语言和首次引导状态等本机偏好。

iPhone 和 iPad 的系统备份可能按设备设置包含应用数据。Android 版关闭了应用的自动云备份，部分设备仍可能在设备迁移时复制应用数据。系统备份与迁移由设备或系统服务提供方管理。

04

系统权限

⌁

相机

仅在你选择拍摄现场照片时使用。拒绝后仍可使用不依赖相机的功能。

▧

照片

仅在你选择从系统相册导入照片时使用。所选照片会被复制到本 App 的私有目录。

iPhone 和 iPad 通过系统权限提示或选择器访问相机和照片。Android 通过系统相机或照片选择器处理你选择的操作，通过系统文件选择器读取你选定的备份文件。你可以拒绝权限请求或取消选择。

05

分享与备份

PDF 和 .prbackup 文件都在设备上生成。你可用系统分享面板把它们交给选定的接收方、文件位置或服务。Android 使用临时文件读取授权提供分享副本。

文件导出后，将由你选择的存储位置、接收方或第三方服务按照其自身隐私政策处理。请在分享前检查 PDF 或备份中是否包含地址、联系人、照片或其他不希望披露的信息。

.prbackup 包含项目、记录、照片和标注。本 App 不会加密该文件，也不会把已生成的 PDF 放进该文件。恢复备份会替换当前本机的项目、记录和照片，恢复前会请求确认。

06

保留与删除

本 App 不会按固定期限自动删除项目和记录。删除项目或记录时，本 App 会移除对应的数据库记录，并清理受其管理的照片副本。删除项目也会清理该项目当前关联的 PDF。

卸载会移除本机的应用私有数据。删除记录或卸载不会删除原相册照片、另存的 PDF 或备份、已分享文件或系统备份里的副本。你可在对应的位置或服务中管理这些副本。

07

第三方系统能力与官网数据

本 App 使用系统相机、照片和文件选择器、PDF 预览与系统分享能力。如果所选照片、备份文件或分享目的地由云服务提供，该服务可能按自身规则获取、传输或存储相关数据。

本官网是无账号、无表单的静态网页。站点脚本将显示语言存入当前浏览器的本地存储，不加载广告或分析脚本。提供网页的基础设施可能处理 IP 地址、访问时间、请求路径和浏览器标识等日志信息以传输网页及维护服务。托管方式及日志处理细节待确认。

08

安全

本 App 使用 iOS 和 Android 的应用沙盒存储记录。请根据资料的敏感程度选择设备安全设置、备份位置与分享对象。

09

儿童信息

JOVFX 面向日常现场记录，未设置面向儿童的账号或服务。如果你将儿童信息写入记录或导入包含儿童的照片，应用会按本政策所述的方式处理这些内容。

Scope

This policy applies to JOVFX (the “App”) on iPhone, iPad, and Android and to this static product website. The App organizes site photos, annotations, numbers, and notes into records.

03

Storage and use

The App stores projects, records, photos, and annotations in the App sandbox on your device. The current recording, markup, and export features work without an account or an App-operated sync service and have no workflow that sends records to developer servers.

The App handles information on your device only to:

  • create, edit, search, and display projects and site records;
  • import, copy, group, and annotate before and after photos;
  • assign stable project record numbers and store status, responsibility, and due dates;
  • generate, preview, and reopen PDFs on device;
  • create or restore a local backup at your direction; and
  • save device preferences such as display language and onboarding status.

System backups on iPhone and iPad may include App data depending on your device settings. The Android App disables automatic cloud backup for App data. Some devices can still copy App data during device-to-device transfer. The device or system service provider manages those backups and transfers.

04

System permissions

⌁

Camera

Used only when you choose to take a site photo. Other features remain available if you deny access.

▧

Photos

Used only when you choose to import a photo from the system photo library. The selected image is copied into the App's private directory.

On iPhone and iPad, the App uses system permission prompts or pickers to access the camera and photos. On Android, it uses the system camera or photo picker for the action you select and the system file picker to read the backup file you choose. You can decline a permission request or cancel a selection.

05

Sharing and backup

The App generates PDF and .prbackup files on your device. You can send them to the recipient, file location, or service you choose in the system share sheet. On Android, the App grants temporary read access to the share copy.

After export, the storage location, recipient, or third-party service you select handles the file under its own privacy policy. Before sharing, review whether a PDF or backup contains addresses, contacts, photos, or other details you do not wish to disclose.

A .prbackup file contains projects, records, photos, and annotations. The App does not encrypt this file or include generated PDFs in it. Restoring a backup replaces the current projects, records, and photos on the device. The App asks for confirmation before restoring.

06

Retention and deletion

The App does not automatically delete projects or records after a fixed period. When you delete a project or record, the App removes its database entries and cleans up the copies of its photos managed by the App. Deleting a project also cleans up its currently linked PDF.

Uninstalling removes private App data on that device. Deleting a record or uninstalling does not remove photos from your original library, saved PDFs or backups, shared copies, or copies in system backups. You can manage those copies in the applicable location or service.

07

Third-party system services and website data

The App uses the system camera, photo and file pickers, PDF preview, and system sharing. If a cloud service supplies a photo or backup you choose or provides the share destination, that service may retrieve, transfer, or store the data under its own terms.

This static website has no accounts or forms. The site script saves your display language in your current browser's local storage and loads no advertising or analytics scripts. Infrastructure that serves the website may process connection logs, such as IP addresses, access times, request paths, and browser identifiers, for delivery and service operations. Hosting and log-handling details are pending confirmation.

08

Security

The App stores records in the application sandbox on iOS and Android. Choose device security settings, backup locations, and recipients that suit the sensitivity of your records.

09

Children's information

JOVFX is a general site documentation tool and provides no accounts or services directed at children. If you enter information about children or import a photo that includes them, the App handles that content as described in this policy.

10

Changes to this policy

When features, permissions, or data handling change, we will update this policy and its effective date on this page.
