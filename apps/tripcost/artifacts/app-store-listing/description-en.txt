RoamSum helps you understand the real cost of travel purchases before and after you pay.

Convert local prices with daily reference exchange rates, then keep working with cached data when a connection is unavailable. Scan a price tag, menu, or receipt with on-device text recognition, or enter an amount manually.

Compare payment methods using the rules that matter to you, including foreign transaction fees, cross-border fees, rate markups, fixed charges, and cashback. Check a merchant's dynamic currency conversion offer against the reference cost so the extra markup is easier to see.

Build trips with multiple destinations, dates, currencies, and budgets. Record expenses in the original currency, preserve the rate and payment-rule snapshot used for each entry, and add the final posted amount later. The ledger shows category totals and helps you see how much of the trip budget remains.

RoamSum also lets you:

• Manage reusable payment methods and fee rules
• Save receipts with their related expenses
• Export expense data as CSV or PDF
• Create local backup files
• Save a trip summary image to your photo library
• Use English or Simplified Chinese

No account is required. Trips, expenses, settings, and receipt images stay on your device by default. Receipt recognition runs on device. On Apple platforms, optional iCloud sync stores supported structured data in your private CloudKit database; original receipt images are excluded. Android does not offer iCloud sync. Reference-rate lookups use the Frankfurter public API.

Exchange rates and cost estimates are for reference only and are not financial or investment advice. Final charges depend on the merchant, card network, issuer, payment provider, and posting date.
