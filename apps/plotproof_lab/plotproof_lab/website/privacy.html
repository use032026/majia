先说重点

PlotProof Lab 当前版本无需账号，核心实验可离线使用，学习记录默认只保存在设备本地。应用不请求系统权限，不包含广告、行为分析、跨应用追踪或第三方 AI SDK。联网只用于读取经校验的公开数据题目包，不上传作答、进度或语言偏好。

1. 适用范围与处理主体

本政策适用于 PlotProof Lab（图证实验室）移动应用及其官方静态网站。应用商店、操作系统、下载渠道、浏览器、域名解析和网站托管服务商是各自独立的服务提供者，其处理活动受各自条款与隐私政策约束。

2. 隐私摘要

  • 无需账号：不提供注册、登录、用户档案或开发者云端空间。
  • 本地优先：作答历史、正确性、完成时间、误区标签和语言偏好保存在当前设备。
  • 核心功能离线：八个随包实验不需要网络；远程题库不可用时仍可继续学习。
  • 无系统权限：当前版本不请求相机、相册、麦克风、位置、联系人、蓝牙或通知权限。
  • 无广告与追踪：当前项目代码未集成广告、行为分析、跨应用追踪或第三方 AI SDK。
  • 不上传学习记录：题库请求不包含作答、进度、语言偏好、账号标识或用户输入。

4. 题库更新与网络访问

核心实验随应用安装包提供。应用可至多每七天自动检查一次版本化题目包；你也可以在“设置 → 题库更新”主动检查。当前默认地址是 GitHub Raw 上的只读 HTTPS JSON。

应用只接受受支持的 schema、合法参数、唯一远程 ID、HTTPS 公开来源和匹配 SHA-256 校验值的题目。单次响应大小限制为 1 MiB，单个下载包最多包含 8 道题；校验或下载失败不会替换现有有效内容。

题库请求不附带本地作答、正确率、误区、语言偏好或用户输入。GitHub、域名解析、网络运营商及其他传输基础设施仍可能按其规则处理传送请求所需的 IP 地址、时间和技术日志。

6. 信息共享与第三方处理

我们不出售个人信息，不为广告投放共享学习记录，也不把本地作答上传给开发者。当前版本没有应用自有账号服务器或同步服务。

  • 题目包托管与网络：GitHub 及传输基础设施可能处理完成 HTTPS 请求所需的连接与安全日志。
  • 公开数据来源：题目可展示 Our World in Data、World Bank 等公开来源的归属、许可和原始 URL；应用不会向这些来源发送你的学习记录。
  • 应用商店与操作系统：安装、更新、系统备份、崩溃诊断或商店分析可能由平台独立处理；应用未接入开发者自有分析服务。
  • 网站基础设施：正式部署后，托管、域名解析或网络防护服务可能处理连接和安全日志。
  • 法律要求：如适用法律要求且开发者实际持有相关信息，我们可能依法响应有权机关的有效要求。

7. 信息保存与删除

  • 学习尝试会保存在设备上，直到你在“设置 → 清除全部学习记录”中确认删除、清除应用数据或卸载应用。
  • “清除全部学习记录”删除作答历史、完成状态和错题队列；它不会重置语言、引导状态或已缓存的公开题目包。
  • 语言、引导状态、题库缓存和更新检查时间可通过清除应用数据或卸载应用移除。
  • 操作系统备份、整机迁移或你启用的云端设备备份可能复制应用数据；这些副本由设备和平台设置控制，开发者无法直接访问。
  • 网站语言偏好可通过清除本站浏览器存储删除；网站日志期限取决于最终部署配置和服务商规则。

8. 本网站与外部链接

本网站为静态页面，不设置广告 Cookie 或跨站追踪器。语言选择保存在浏览器 localStorage，键名为 plotproof-site-language。网站脚本不会把该值发送给开发者。

浏览器请求页面时，托管和网络基础设施仍可能产生常规连接日志。题目来源 URL、邮件链接及未来的商店链接会带你离开本网站；外部服务适用其自身条款和隐私规则。

9. 你的控制与权利

由于核心学习数据默认由你在设备上持有，你可以直接：

  • 查看学习进度、错题队列和误区记录；
  • 重新完成关卡，以更新最近一次掌握状态；
  • 在设置中确认后清除全部学习尝试；
  • 不使用动态题库，仅使用随包离线实验；
  • 清除应用数据或卸载应用，以移除应用本地数据；
  • 通过本政策列明的邮箱咨询隐私问题或行使适用法律赋予的权利。

因为开发者不持有设备上的学习记录，我们通常无法从服务器替你搜索、导出、修改或恢复这些记录。

10. 安全、儿童与产品边界

本地优先可以减少数据离开设备的机会，但不能消除设备丢失、弱密码、越狱/root、恶意软件、系统备份或他人接触设备等风险。请使用设备锁并保持操作系统更新。

应用不要求儿童或其他用户提交身份、联系方式或敏感信息，也没有社交、公开发布或用户内容上传功能。监护人和教育工作者应结合适用地区要求决定是否由未成年人使用。

PlotProof Lab 训练数据识读能力，不判断真实新闻真伪，也不提供医疗、投资、法律或统计专业建议。公开数据题目与解析由 PlotProof Lab 生成，不代表数据提供方观点。

4. Lesson updates and network access

Core labs ship with the app. The app may automatically check a versioned lesson pack at most once every seven days, and you can also check from Settings → Lesson updates. The current default endpoint is read-only HTTPS JSON hosted on GitHub Raw.

The app accepts only a supported schema, valid parameters, unique remote IDs, HTTPS public sources, and a matching SHA-256 checksum. A response is limited to 1 MiB, and one downloaded pack may contain at most eight lessons. A failed download or validation does not replace existing valid content.

The request carries no local attempt, accuracy, misconception, language preference, or user input. GitHub, DNS, network operators, and other transmission infrastructure may still process the IP address, time, and technical logs needed to deliver the request under their rules.

6. Sharing and third-party processing

We do not sell personal information, share learning records for advertising, or upload local attempts to the developer. The current version has no first-party account server or synchronization service.

  • Lesson hosting and network: GitHub and transmission infrastructure may process connection and security logs needed to complete an HTTPS request.
  • Public-data sources: lessons may display attribution, licenses, and original URLs for sources such as Our World in Data and the World Bank. The app does not send learning records to those sources.
  • App stores and operating systems: platforms may independently handle installation, updates, system backup, crash diagnostics, or store analytics. The app includes no developer-operated analytics service.
  • Website infrastructure: hosting, DNS, and network-protection providers may process connection and security logs after public deployment.
  • Legal requirements: if applicable law requires it and the developer actually holds relevant information, we may respond to a valid request from a competent authority.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Learning attempts remain on the device until you confirm Settings → Clear all learning progress, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Clear all learning progress removes attempt history, completion state, and the review queue. It does not reset language, onboarding state, or the cached public lesson pack.
  • Remove language, onboarding state, lesson cache, and update timestamps by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • System backup, device migration, or a cloud device backup you enable may copy app data. Those copies are controlled by device and platform settings and are not directly accessible to the developer.
  • Remove the website language preference by clearing this site's browser storage. Website-log retention depends on the final deployment and provider rules.

8. This website and external links

This is a static website. It sets no advertising cookie or cross-site tracker. Language selection is stored in browser localStorage under plotproof-site-language, and the site script does not send that value to the developer.

Normal connection logs may still arise in hosting and network infrastructure when a browser requests the page. Lesson-source URLs, email links, and future store links take you outside this site, where the destination's own terms and privacy rules apply.

9. Your controls and rights

Because core learning data stays on your device by default, you can directly:

  • view learning progress, the review queue, and misconception history;
  • complete a lesson again to update its latest mastery state;
  • clear all learning attempts after confirmation in Settings;
  • avoid dynamic lessons and use only bundled offline labs;
  • clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app data; and
  • contact the email below with a privacy question or to exercise an applicable legal right.

Because the developer does not hold on-device learning records, we normally cannot search, export, modify, or restore those records from a server.

10. Security, children, and product limits

Local-first design reduces opportunities for data to leave the device, but it cannot remove risks such as device loss, weak passcodes, jailbreak/root access, malware, system backups, or another person accessing the device. Use a device lock and keep the operating system current.

The app does not ask children or other users for identity, contact, or sensitive information and has no social, public-posting, or user-content upload feature. Parents, guardians, and educators should decide whether use by a minor is appropriate under local requirements.

PlotProof Lab teaches data literacy. It does not verify real news and does not provide medical, investment, legal, or professional statistical advice. Public-data lessons and explanations are generated by PlotProof Lab and do not represent the data provider's views.
