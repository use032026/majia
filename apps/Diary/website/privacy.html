先说重点

回声页当前版本无需账号，没有开发者后台、广告、行为分析、远程配置或应用内购买，也不主动联网。日记内容默认保存在设备上的应用沙盒中，不会发送给开发者。

1. 适用范围与负责方

本政策适用于“回声页 · EchoPage”移动应用及其官方静态网站。应用商店页面、安装包信息或开发过程中的内部名称可能有所不同，应以实际发布的应用版本为准。

2. 隐私摘要

  • 无需账号：当前版本没有注册、登录、用户资料或开发者云空间。
  • 本地优先：日记正文、标题、未来问题、后记、变化标记和设置默认保存在应用沙盒。
  • 无运行时权限：当前版本不申请相机、照片、麦克风、位置、通知、通讯录或跟踪权限。
  • 无广告或行为分析：当前版本不含广告 SDK、第三方行为分析 SDK、跨应用跟踪或第三方 AI。
  • 由你发起复制：只有主动选择“复制完整记录”时，内容才会写入系统剪贴板。
  • 删除边界明确：永久删除可清理当前安装中的应用文件，但不能替你清除历史设备或云端系统备份。

4. 权限与网络

当前版本不申请相机、照片、麦克风、位置、通知、通讯录或跟踪权限，也没有应用自有后台，不主动联网。开发或调试构建可能包含平台工具所需的调试网络能力；该能力不代表面向用户的发布版本会上传日记。

如果未来增加同步、通知、图片、语音、位置、分析、广告或其他联网功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策、应用内说明和相应商店隐私信息。

5. 本地存储与可靠性

日记快照以 JSON 文件保存在应用的 Documents 沙盒。为降低写入中断导致的数据损坏风险，应用在保存时使用待写文件，并保留上一版可读的应用内备份。主文件不可读时，应用可以读取上一版备份并明确提示；主文件和备份均不可读时，应用会锁定编辑，避免用空数据覆盖原内容。

这些机制属于当前安装内部的可靠性保护，不是开发者云同步，也不提供跨设备恢复。

6. 剪贴板、共享与第三方

回声页不会自动导出或发送日记。只有当你主动选择“复制完整记录”时，应用才会把包含原文、未来问题、回声日期和变化标记的纯文本写入系统剪贴板。剪贴板内容随后可能被操作系统或你选择的其他应用读取，具体行为受其各自隐私政策和系统设置约束。

当前版本不提供开发者自有云同步、自动备份、文件导入或文件分享，也不包含广告、行为分析、远程配置或第三方 AI 服务。

7. 保留、删除与系统备份边界

  • 内容保留在当前安装的应用沙盒中，直到你在应用内删除或清空数据，或者按平台行为卸载应用。
  • 普通删除先将页面移入“最近删除”，你可以恢复；永久删除、清空最近删除和清空全部需要确认，完成后无法在应用内撤销。
  • 永久删除和清空会清理当前安装中的主数据文件、待写文件与应用内备份中的相关内容。
  • iOS / Android 的设备备份、iCloud / Google 备份、设备迁移或恢复可能根据系统设置包含应用沙盒数据。开发者无法读取、管理或替你删除这些历史系统备份。

因此，“永久删除”仅承诺净化当前安装中由应用管理的数据，不承诺删除操作系统或第三方接收应用已经保留的历史副本。

8. 网站数据

本网站是无账号、无表单的静态网站，不使用广告、行为分析或跟踪脚本，也不设置广告 Cookie。为了记住中英文选择，网站会在浏览器的 localStorage 中保存一个语言偏好；你可以随时通过浏览器设置清除。

网站部署后，托管商和网络基础设施可能为了传输页面、安全防护、故障排查和服务运行而处理 IP 地址、请求时间、浏览器类型与错误日志等常规连接数据。其范围、位置和保留时间取决于实际采用的托管服务。

9. 安全与内容性质

我们通过本地沙盒、原子式写入、可读备份恢复和写入失败不提交等方式降低意外损坏风险，但任何设备存储都无法保证绝对安全。请使用系统密码、生物识别、设备加密和备份设置保护设备。

回声页是个人记录工具，不提供医疗、心理诊断、治疗或紧急援助服务。

10. 儿童隐私

回声页不以儿童为目标，也不要求年龄或儿童信息。若监护人认为儿童在日记中写入了不适当的信息，可在当前设备上删除对应页面或清空应用数据，并在系统设置中管理设备备份。

11. 你的选择与权利

因为当前版本没有账号或开发者服务器，开发者通常不持有可供检索、下载、更正或删除的日记副本。你可以直接在应用内查看、编辑、搜索、复制、移到最近删除、恢复、永久删除或清空当前安装中的内容，并可在设备设置中管理系统备份和卸载应用。

如果你对本政策或数据边界有疑问，可以通过下方邮箱联系我们。为保护隐私，请不要在邮件中发送不必要的完整日记内容。

12. 政策变更

应用的数据流、权限、依赖、商业模式或网站服务发生变化时，我们会更新本政策并修改页面顶部的生效日期。重大变化会在应用内或发布渠道中以适当方式提示。

The current EchoPage app requires no account and has no developer backend, ads, behavioral analytics, remote configuration, or in-app purchases. It does not actively connect to the network. Journal content stays in the app sandbox on your device by default and is not sent to the developer.

2. Privacy summary

  • No account: the current version has no registration, sign-in, user profile, or developer-hosted cloud space.
  • Local-first: pages, titles, future questions, Echoes, perspective markers, and settings stay in the app sandbox by default.

  • No ads or behavioral analytics: the current version includes no ad SDK, third-party behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, or third-party AI.
  • You initiate copying: content reaches the system clipboard only when you choose Copy complete record.
  • Clear deletion boundary: permanent deletion can clear app-managed files in the current installation, but it cannot remove historical device or cloud system backups for you.

4. Permissions and network access

The current version does not request camera, photos, microphone, location, notifications, contacts, or tracking permissions. It has no developer backend and does not actively connect to the network. Development or debug builds may contain networking capability required by platform tooling; that does not mean the user-facing release uploads journals.

If a future version adds sync, notifications, images, voice, location, analytics, advertising, or another networked feature, we will update this policy, in-app notices, and relevant store privacy information before release.

5. Local storage and reliability

Journal snapshots are stored as JSON in the app's Documents sandbox. To reduce the risk of interrupted writes, the app uses a pending file while saving and retains the last readable in-app backup. If the current file is unreadable, the app can load the prior backup and show an explicit notice. If both are unreadable, editing is locked so empty data does not overwrite the original content.

These mechanisms protect reliability within the current installation. They are not developer cloud sync and do not provide cross-device recovery.

6. Clipboard, sharing, and third parties

EchoPage does not automatically export or transmit journals. Only when you choose Copy complete record does the app place plain text—including the original page, future question, Echo dates, and perspective markers—on the system clipboard. The operating system or other apps you select may then read that content under their own policies and your system settings.

The current version offers no developer cloud sync, automatic backup, file import, or file sharing, and it includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, remote configuration, or third-party AI service.

7. Retention, deletion, and system-backup boundaries

  • Content remains in the current app installation until you delete or clear it in the app, or uninstall the app according to platform behavior.
  • Ordinary deletion first moves a page to Recently Deleted, where it can be restored. Permanent deletion, emptying Recently Deleted, and Clear All require confirmation and cannot be undone in the app.
  • Permanent deletion and clearing remove relevant content from the current installation's main, pending, and in-app backup files.
  • Device backups, iCloud or Google backups, device migration, and restore may include app sandbox data depending on platform settings. The developer cannot read, manage, or delete those historical system backups for you.

“Permanent deletion” therefore means sanitizing data managed by the current installation. It does not promise deletion from operating-system backups or copies already held by another app.

8. Website data

This is a static website with no account or form. It uses no advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking scripts and sets no advertising cookies. To remember Chinese or English, it stores one language preference in browser localStorage; you can remove it through browser settings.

Once deployed, the hosting provider and network infrastructure may process ordinary connection data—such as IP address, request time, browser type, and error logs—to deliver pages, protect and operate the service, and troubleshoot faults. The exact scope, location, and retention depend on the hosting service actually selected.

9. Security and the nature of the content

We reduce accidental corruption risks through the local sandbox, atomic-style writes, readable backup recovery, and save-before-commit behavior, but no device storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Use your device passcode, biometrics, encryption, and backup settings to protect the device.

EchoPage is a personal journaling tool. It does not provide medical or psychological diagnosis, treatment, or emergency assistance.

10. Children's privacy

EchoPage is not directed to children and does not ask for age or children's information. If a guardian believes a child has entered inappropriate information, they can delete the relevant page or clear app data on the current device and manage device backups in system settings.

11. Your choices and rights

Because the current version has no account or developer server, the developer generally has no journal copy to retrieve, download, correct, or delete. In the app, you can view, edit, search, copy, move to Recently Deleted, restore, permanently delete, or clear content in the current installation. You can also manage system backups and uninstall the app through device settings.

If you have a question about this policy or these data boundaries, contact us below. To protect your privacy, do not include unnecessary complete journal entries in your email.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and the effective date above when the app's data flow, permissions, dependencies, business model, or website services change. We will provide an appropriate notice in the app or release channel for material changes.
