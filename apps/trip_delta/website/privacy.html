Privacy Policy

Trip Delta is a travel budget app operated by 独立开发者. This policy describes the current app's handling of trip data and the optional actions that can send information to services you choose.

Information you enter

You may enter trip names, destinations, dates, category budgets, expenses, notes, exchange rates, final posted amounts, and reflections. The app also saves your language choice. These records can reveal personal spending patterns, so take care when exporting or sharing them. The inspected app has no account system, developer backend, advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud sync. Its trip functions do not upload your entries to the operator.

Storage and device backup

Trip records and settings are stored as JSON in the app's private Application Support area on your device. The operating system may include those files in a device backup, depending on your backup settings and platform. A device backup is managed by you and the platform, not by an automatic Trip Delta sync service. If you do not export a backup, uninstalling the app may erase your local records.

Permissions and device features

Trip planning and expense entry do not request camera, location, microphone, or contacts access. When you choose to import or export a JSON backup, the app opens the system file picker or save interface. When you choose to save a review card, the app writes an image to your photo library and may request the relevant photo-add permission on iOS or storage-write permission on older Android versions. When you choose to share a card, the system share sheet opens so you can pick a destination.

Network and other services

The app uses the internet permission on Android for its optional online privacy page. If an HTTPS policy URL is configured and you open that page, a web view requests the page; it does not attach your trip records. The website host may receive ordinary access information such as IP address, browser details, and request time. The operator will confirm the host and its log retention before publishing the website. File providers, photo libraries, device backup services, and apps you choose through the share sheet process the items you send under their own terms. Exchange rates are entered by you; the app does not fetch live quotes.

Retention and deletion

Local trip records remain until you delete an expense or trip, replace all records with an imported backup, or remove the app. Deleting a trip removes it from the current app data. Trip Delta cannot remove copies already present in device backups, files you exported, images saved to Photos, or destinations to which you shared a card. You manage those copies in the relevant service. The app deletes its temporary share image after the share sheet closes.

Children

Trip Delta is a general travel budgeting tool. It does not provide child accounts, targeted advertising, or a way for the operator to receive trip entries through the app. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of file export and sharing features as they would other device sharing tools.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy or support questions, contact 独立开发者 at yamenayazman@icloud.com.

隐私政策

Trip Delta 是由“独立开发者”运营的旅行预算应用。本政策说明当前版本如何处理旅程数据，以及哪些由你主动选择的操作会将信息交给其他服务。

你输入的信息

你可以输入旅程名称、目的地、日期、分类预算、支出、备注、汇率、最终入账金额和复盘内容。应用还保存你的语言选择。这些记录可能反映个人消费情况，导出或分享时请谨慎。经检查的当前应用没有账号系统、开发者后台、广告、分析功能或自动云同步；旅程功能不会把你的记录上传给运营者。

本机存储与设备备份

旅程记录和设置以 JSON 文件保存在设备上的应用私有 Application Support 区域。是否进入设备系统备份取决于平台和你的备份设置。设备备份由你和平台管理，并非 Trip Delta 自动同步服务。如果没有主动导出备份，卸载应用可能清除本机记录。

权限与设备功能

规划旅程和记录支出不请求相机、位置、麦克风或联系人权限。你主动导入或导出 JSON 备份时，应用会打开系统文件选择或保存界面。你主动把回顾卡片保存到相册时，应用会写入图片；iOS 可能请求添加照片权限，较旧的 Android 版本可能请求存储写入权限。你主动分享卡片时，系统分享界面会让你选择接收方。

网络与其他服务

Android 应用声明互联网权限，供可选的在线隐私政策页使用。如果已配置 HTTPS 政策网址且你打开该页面，网页视图会请求该网页，但不会附上旅程记录。网站托管服务可能获得 IP 地址、浏览器信息和请求时间等常规访问信息。官网发布前，运营者会确认托管方和日志保留方式。你选择的文件服务、相册、设备备份服务和系统分享目标会按各自规则处理你交给它们的内容。汇率由你手动输入，应用不获取实时汇率。

保留与删除

本机旅程记录会保留，直到你删除支出或旅程、导入备份并选择替换全部记录，或移除应用。删除旅程会从当前应用数据中移除它。Trip Delta 无法删除设备备份、已导出的文件、保存到相册的图片或已分享目标中的副本；这些副本由你在相应服务中管理。系统分享界面关闭后，应用会删除用于分享的临时图片。

儿童使用

Trip Delta 是通用旅行预算工具，不提供儿童账号或定向广告，也没有通过应用将旅程记录交给运营者的功能。家长或监护人应像管理设备上其他分享工具一样，指导儿童使用文件导出与分享功能。

政策变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在本页面公布并更新生效日期。如有隐私或支持问题，请联系“独立开发者”：yamenayazman@icloud.com。
