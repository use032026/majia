Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current Jufu 1.0 mobile app and its static product website handle information. Jufu is operated by Jufu独立开发者. Questions can be sent to djl13333995679@163.com. This product-specific policy is not legal advice.

Information and data

Jufu does not require an account or sign-in. When you choose a photo through the operating system, the app reads that selected image so it can show a preview, determine its dimensions, apply crop, zoom, effects, brightness, and watermark settings, and generate a result. The selected photo, watermark text, and edit settings are used in the app's local runtime and are not uploaded to a developer server by the current implementation.

The app stores your language choice and whether onboarding has been completed in local preferences. It does not save edits as a recoverable project library. The website separately stores only the selected site language, “en” or “zh”, in browser localStorage.

Device permissions

Photo selection access lets you choose one image through the system photo interface. Add-to-Photos or storage access is used only when you ask Jufu to save a generated image. On iOS, the app requests add-only Photo Library authorization for saving; the system photo picker controls selection. On Android 10 and later, the app writes through MediaStore, while Android 9 and earlier may use the declared storage permission. The current app does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, notifications, health, Bluetooth, or App Tracking Transparency permission.

Network and third-party services

The current app includes no developer API client, account service, cloud editing, advertising SDK, or behavioral analytics SDK. Its Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links open lunelleglobal.com in your external browser. Your browser, network provider, DNS, hosting, CDN, and security providers may process ordinary connection data such as IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs according to the production hosting configuration and their own policies. Any such retention follows the applicable production configuration and provider policies.

Apple, Google, an app store, the operating system, a device manufacturer, a cloud photo service, or a backup service may independently process data under its own settings and policy. Their processing is outside Jufu's local editor.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Unsaved editing state exists only for the current app session and may disappear when you replace the photo, close the app, or the operating system clears temporary files. Photo-picker components may create temporary local files governed by the operating system or app sandbox. Language and onboarding preferences remain until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall Jufu.

Images that you save remain in the system photo library until you delete them there. Uninstalling Jufu does not delete saved images or copies maintained by device backup or cloud photo services. The website language preference remains until browser site data is cleared. Production website logs, if enabled, follow the final hosting configuration and applicable requirements.

Exports and sharing

When you tap Save to Photos, Jufu renders PNG data on the device and asks the operating system to add the generated image to the photo library. The app does not automatically overwrite the original and has no built-in upload or social-sharing flow in the current version. You control any later export or sharing through the photo library, operating system, or another app. The current implementation does not sell photos, watermark text, edit settings, or local preferences and does not use them for cross-app tracking.

Children's privacy

Jufu is a general photo-editing tool and is not directed to children. The current app does not create user profiles or knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer service. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's device, photo-library permissions, saved images, backups, and any later sharing. Contact us if you believe the released service handles a child's information in a way that requires action.

Your choices and requests

You decide whether to select a photo, change an edit, and save a generated image. You can change or withdraw photo permissions in system settings, remove saved images from the photo library, change the app language, clear local app or browser data, and uninstall the app. Because Jufu has no account or developer-hosted artwork library, the operator ordinarily cannot locate, export, correct, or delete photos kept only on your device by using your name or email.

Changes to this policy

We will reassess this policy before releasing a feature that changes data flows, such as accounts, cloud sync, online processing, camera capture, analytics, advertising, payments, or built-in sharing. Material changes may be communicated through the website, app, store release notes, or another method required by law. The date at the top identifies this policy's effective date.

隐私政策

本政策说明 Jufu 1.0 当前移动应用及其静态产品网站如何处理信息。Jufu 的运营主体为 Jufu独立开发者；如有问题，请发送邮件至 djl13333995679@163.com。本文件是面向产品的隐私政策，不构成法律意见。

信息与数据

Jufu 不要求注册账号或登录。当你通过操作系统选择一张照片后，应用会读取这张明确选中的图片，用于显示预览、获取尺寸、应用裁剪、缩放、效果、亮度与水印设置，并生成最终图片。当前实现只在应用本地运行环境中使用选中的照片、水印文字和编辑参数，不会把这些内容上传到开发者服务器。

应用会在本地偏好中保存你的界面语言选择和是否已完成引导，不会把编辑内容保存为可恢复的项目库。网站会另外在浏览器 localStorage 中保存所选网站语言，值仅为“en”或“zh”。

设备权限

照片选择权限用于通过系统照片界面选择一张图片；添加到照片或存储权限只在你要求 Jufu 保存生成图片时使用。iOS 保存时会请求仅添加到照片图库的授权，照片选择范围由系统选择器控制。Android 10 及以上版本通过 MediaStore 写入图片，Android 9 及以下版本可能使用已声明的存储权限。当前应用不请求相机、麦克风、精确位置、联系人、通知、健康、蓝牙或 App Tracking Transparency 权限。

网络与第三方服务

当前应用未集成开发者 API 客户端、账号服务、云端编辑、广告 SDK 或行为分析 SDK。应用中的隐私政策与用户协议链接会在外部浏览器打开 lunelleglobal.com。浏览器、网络运营商、DNS、托管、CDN 和安全服务商可能按照正式部署配置及其政策处理 IP 地址、请求时间、浏览器信息和安全日志等常规连接数据；如有保留，其期限遵循实际生产配置和服务商政策。

Apple、Google、应用商店、操作系统、设备厂商、云相册或备份服务也可能依据各自设置和政策独立处理数据；这类处理不属于 Jufu 本地编辑器本身。

存储、保留与删除

未保存的编辑状态只用于当前应用会话；当你更换照片、关闭应用或系统清理临时文件时，这些状态可能消失。系统照片选择组件可能创建临时本地文件，并遵循操作系统或应用沙盒的生命周期。语言与引导完成偏好会一直保留，直至你修改设置、清除应用数据或卸载 Jufu。

你主动保存的图片会保留在系统照片库中，直至你自行删除。卸载 Jufu 不会删除已经保存的图片，也不会删除设备备份或云相册保存的副本。网站语言偏好会保留到你清除浏览器站点数据为止。若正式网站启用访问或安全日志，其保留方式取决于最终托管配置和适用要求。

导出与共享

当你点击“保存到相册”时，Jufu 会在设备本地生成 PNG 数据，并请求操作系统把生成图片添加到照片库。应用不会自动覆盖原图，当前版本也没有内置上传或社交分享流程。之后是否导出或分享，由你通过照片库、操作系统或其他应用决定。当前实现不会出售照片、水印文字、编辑参数或本地偏好，也不会利用这些内容进行跨应用追踪。

儿童隐私

Jufu 是通用照片编辑工具，并非面向儿童设计。当前应用不创建用户档案，也不会通过开发者服务主动收集儿童个人信息。家长或监护人应监督儿童设备、照片库权限、保存图片、备份及后续分享。如你认为正式发布的服务以需要处理的方式涉及儿童信息，请联系我们。

你的选择与请求

你可以决定是否选择照片、是否修改编辑内容以及是否保存生成图片；也可以在系统设置中修改或撤回照片权限，在照片库删除已保存图片，修改应用语言，清除应用或浏览器本地数据，或卸载应用。由于 Jufu 没有账号或开发者云端作品库，运营者通常无法仅凭你的姓名或邮箱查找、导出、更正或删除只保存在你设备上的照片与编辑状态。

政策变更

如果未来版本新增账号、云同步、在线处理、相机拍摄、分析、广告、支付或内置分享等会改变数据流的功能，我们会在上线前重新评估并更新本政策。重大变更可能通过网站、应用、商店更新说明或法律要求的其他方式通知。页面顶部日期为本政策的生效日期。
