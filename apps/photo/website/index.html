Your photo stays in your flow

Edit locally. Save deliberately.

Jufu starts only after you choose a photo through the system picker. The current app has no account, developer-hosted artwork library, advertising SDK, or behavioral analytics SDK. It writes a generated image to the system photo library only after you tap Save to Photos.

照片由你掌控

本地编辑，主动保存。

只有当你通过系统照片选择器选中图片后，Jufu 才会开始编辑。当前应用没有账号、开发者云端作品库、广告 SDK 或行为分析 SDK；只有你点击“保存到相册”后，生成图片才会写入系统照片库。
