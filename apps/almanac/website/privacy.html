Privacy Policy

Almanac is operated by an independent developer. This policy has an effective date of October 19, 2026 and describes the current source implementation. The production URL and final signed build must be confirmed before this draft is used publicly.

What the app does

Almanac is a daily reflection tool with no account. On-device rules compose a prompt and short verse. You can choose one Try and one Skip, add an optional intention, seal the leaf, and record a later reflection.

Information and storage

The app stores a local installation seed, daily leaves, your selections, optional intention text, reflection outcomes, language and appearance preferences, and onboarding state using local app storage. The installation seed keeps the same installation's daily content stable. The intention is free text; avoid entering sensitive information you do not want on the device or in system backups. Current source does not send these records to a developer-operated server.

Permissions and services

The current production manifests request no runtime permissions for camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications. The app uses Flutter and a local preferences storage package. Current source has no account service, developer backend, advertising, analytics, or remote AI service. Android debug and profile manifests include Internet permission for development; the final packaged builds and SDK inventory must be checked before publication.

Retention and deletion

App-managed daily records remain locally until you confirm “Clear all daily leaves,” uninstall the app, or the operating system removes app data. Clearing daily leaves resets the current daily leaf and removes prior app-managed leaf records. It keeps language and appearance preferences and the installation seed. Operating-system backups or device migration may preserve copies outside the app's control.

Export and sharing

If you choose “Copy folio,” the app places readable Markdown on the system clipboard. The operating system or an app you paste into may then process that text. This is a readable export, not a restorable backup; Almanac does not automatically share the folio.

Children

Almanac is not specifically directed to children. It does not ask for a child's identity or create a child account. Any free-text intention is entered by the user on the device.

Changes and contact

This draft must be reviewed and updated if the app's features, SDKs, data handling, or publication context changes. For privacy questions, contact the independent developer at CercyServello4757@outlook.com. This page is not yet hosted at a production URL.

隐私政策

Almanac 由独立开发者运营。本政策的生效日期为 2026 年 10 月 19 日，说明当前源码的处理方式。公开使用本草案前，仍须确认正式网址与最终签名包。

应用如何使用

Almanac 是无需账号的每日反思工具。本机规则组合提示和短诗；你可以选一项“宜”和一项“忌”、写可选意图、收下日笺，并在之后记录回看结果。

信息与存储

应用使用本地存储保存安装种子、日笺、你的选择、可选意图文字、回看结果、语言与外观偏好以及首次引导状态。安装种子让同一次安装的每日内容保持稳定。意图为自由文本，请勿写入不希望保存在设备或系统备份中的敏感信息。当前源码不会把这些记录发送到开发者运营的服务器。

权限与服务

当前正式配置未申请相机、照片、麦克风、位置、联系人或通知等运行时权限。应用使用 Flutter 和本地偏好存储组件。当前源码没有账号服务、开发者后台、广告、分析或远程 AI 服务。Android 调试和性能分析配置包含开发用途的联网权限；公开前仍须核对最终打包产物及 SDK 清单。

保留与删除

应用管理的每日记录保存在本地，直到你确认“清除全部日笺”、卸载应用，或操作系统移除应用数据。清除日笺会重置当天日笺并删除先前由应用管理的日笺记录；语言、外观偏好与安装种子会保留。操作系统备份或设备迁移可能在应用控制范围外保留副本。

导出与共享

当你主动选择“复制岁时册”时，应用会把可读的 Markdown 放入系统剪贴板。之后操作系统或你粘贴到的其他应用可能处理这些文字。这是可读导出，不是可恢复备份；Almanac 不会自动分享笺册。

儿童

Almanac 并非专门面向儿童，不要求儿童身份信息，也不建立儿童账号。任何自由文本意图都由用户在设备上输入。

变更与联系

如果应用功能、SDK、数据处理或发布条件改变，本草案须复核更新。如有隐私问题，请通过 CercyServello4757@outlook.com 联系独立开发者。此页面尚未部署到正式网址。
