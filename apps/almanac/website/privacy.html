Privacy Policy

Almanac is a daily reflection app operated by an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your information and how to contact us.

What the app does

Almanac is a daily reflection tool with no account. On-device rules compose a prompt and short verse. You can choose one Try and one Skip, add an optional intention, seal the leaf, and record a later reflection.

Information kept on your device

Almanac stores your daily leaves, choices, any intention you write, reflections, language and appearance settings, and whether you have completed the introduction in the app's local storage. A randomly created value on your device helps keep each day's content consistent for this installation. These records are used to show your daily leaf and folio; Almanac does not send them to the developer. Avoid writing sensitive information that you do not want stored on your device or in a system backup.

Network access and permissions

Almanac's daily features work without an account or internet connection. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or cloud AI services. It does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications.

Retention and deletion

Your records stay on your device until you clear them, uninstall Almanac, or your device removes the app's data. “Clear all daily leaves” deletes saved leaves, choices, intentions, and reflections, then creates a fresh leaf for today. It keeps your language and appearance settings, the local value used to compose daily content, and the completed-introduction setting. System backups or device migration may keep separate copies; manage those through your device settings.

Export and sharing

When you choose “Copy folio,” Almanac puts your saved leaves in readable Markdown on the system clipboard. If you paste that text into another app, that app may handle it under its own privacy practices. Almanac does not automatically share your folio. A copied folio is not a restorable app backup.

Children

Almanac is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask for a child's identity or create accounts.

This website

The language switch on this website remembers your choice in your browser so the pages open in your preferred language.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if how Almanac handles information changes. If you email us, we receive the address and message you send so we can respond; your app records are not included unless you choose to share them. For privacy questions, contact CercyServello4757@outlook.com.

隐私政策

Almanac 是由独立开发者运营的每日反思应用。本政策说明应用如何处理你的信息，以及如何联系我们。

应用如何使用

Almanac 是无需账号的每日反思工具。本机规则组合提示和短诗；你可以选一项“宜”和一项“忌”、写可选意图、收下日笺，并在之后记录回看结果。

保存在设备上的信息

Almanac 在应用本地保存日笺、你的选择、填写的意图、回看结果、语言与外观设置，以及是否已完成首次引导。设备上随机生成的一个数值，用于让同一次安装的每日内容保持一致。这些记录用于展示今日笺和笺册；Almanac 不会将其发送给开发者。请勿写入不希望保存在设备或系统备份中的敏感信息。

联网与权限

Almanac 的日常功能无需账号或网络连接。应用不使用广告、分析或云端 AI 服务，也不会申请相机、照片、麦克风、位置、联系人或通知权限。

保留与删除

记录保存在设备上，直到你清除记录、卸载 Almanac，或设备移除应用数据。“清除全部日笺”会删除已保存的日笺、选择、意图和回看结果，并重新生成当天的日笺。语言与外观设置、用于组合每日内容的本地数值，以及首次引导完成状态会保留。系统备份或设备迁移可能留下独立副本；你可通过设备设置管理这些副本。

导出与共享

当你主动选择“复制岁时册”时，Almanac 会将已保存的日笺以可读的 Markdown 文本放入系统剪贴板。如果你将其粘贴到其他应用，该应用可能按自己的隐私规则处理这些内容。Almanac 不会自动分享笺册；复制的笺册也不是可恢复的应用备份。

儿童

Almanac 并非专为儿童设计，不要求儿童身份信息，也不建立账号。

本网站

本网站的语言切换会在浏览器中记住你的选择，以便下次按所选语言显示页面。

变更与联系

如果 Almanac 处理信息的方式发生变化，我们会更新此页面。若你发送邮件，我们会收到你提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容，以便回复；除非你主动分享，否则应用内记录不会包含在邮件中。如有隐私问题，请联系 CercyServello4757@outlook.com。
