Privacy Policy

Somniloquy is a local-first night recording and manual review app operated by [[OPERATOR]]. This policy explains what the current version stores, how device permissions are used, and which choices remain under your control.

Information and data

Somniloquy has no account system. When you choose to record, the app can create an audio file and store recording times, a bedtime prompt, selected night context, volume-based candidate moments, your labels, and a morning note. The app also stores your language choice, onboarding status, and a recovery marker when needed to avoid treating an interrupted recording as complete. These items are stored in the app sandbox on your device.

Microphone permission and recording consent

Microphone access is requested only after you open the recording explanation, confirm that you have obtained any consent required for people sharing the room, and choose to start. A visible in-app recording indicator and timer remain on screen while the recording view is active. You are responsible for following the recording and consent laws that apply where you use the app.

Network and third-party services

The current version has no operator-controlled backend and does not use app content for analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud sync, payments, or online AI. It does not actively upload recordings, labels, prompts, or notes. Apple provides app distribution and may provide device backup if you enable that system feature; Apple's terms and privacy practices apply to those services.

Storage, retention, and deletion

App content remains in the app sandbox until you delete a night card, delete all local data from Settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting a night card or all local data removes the corresponding app-managed index entries and audio files. Deletion cannot remove copies that may already exist in a device backup controlled by the operating system. The final release build's backup and file-protection behavior should be reviewed against this policy before publication.

Exports and sharing

The current version does not provide an export or sharing feature. Playback occurs from the audio stored on your device. Somniloquy does not sell app content or use it for cross-app tracking.

Health information

Somniloquy does not connect to HealthKit and is not a medical device. Candidate moments indicate relative changes in volume only. They do not determine who or what made a sound and are not conclusions about snoring disorders, apnea, disease, sleep stages, or sleep quality.

Children's privacy

Somniloquy is intended for adults and is not designed for children. The current version has no account or operator-controlled service that knowingly collects a child's personal information. A parent or guardian should decide whether local recording is appropriate and ensure that all required recording consents are obtained.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices or this policy change, the updated policy will be posted at this location with a revised effective date. Material changes to networking, accounts, analytics, advertising, AI, payment, export, sharing, or health integrations require the policy to be reviewed before release.
